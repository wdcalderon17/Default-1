Houston's Personal Injury Law Firm

Houston is the fourth-largest city in the United States and one of the most complex metropolitan areas in the nation. With more than 2.3 million residents in the city proper and nearly 7 million in the Greater Houston area, Houston's roadways, worksites, and public spaces see a staggering number of accidents every year.

Calderon Legal Group is a Houston-based personal injury law firm fighting for accident victims throughout Harris County and the surrounding region. We understand the local courts, the local insurance landscape, and the specific dangers that Houston roads and workplaces present.

Houston's Most Dangerous Roadways

Texas Department of Transportation data consistently identifies these Houston-area roads among the state's most dangerous:

  • I-45 (Gulf Freeway & North Freeway) — among the deadliest interstates in the US
  • I-10 (Katy Freeway) — one of the widest freeways in the world, with heavy traffic and frequent crashes
  • Beltway 8 / Sam Houston Tollway — high-speed outer loop with significant truck traffic
  • US-59 / I-69 — connecting Midtown Houston outward in multiple directions
  • SH-288 (South Freeway)
  • US-290 (Northwest Freeway) toward Cypress and beyond

FAQs: Houston Personal Injury Claims

Texas law gives most injury victims two years from the date of injury to file a lawsuit (Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code § 16.003). Some exceptions apply — for example, claims against government entities have much shorter deadlines. Contact Calderon Legal Group as soon as possible to ensure your rights are preserved.
1) Call 911 and get a police report. 2) Seek medical attention immediately, even if you feel okay. 3) Photograph the scene, vehicles, and your injuries. 4) Get witness contact information. 5) Do NOT give a recorded statement to the at-fault driver's insurance company. 6) Call Calderon Legal Group for a free consultation before taking any further action.
The majority of personal injury cases in Harris County resolve through settlement — without a trial. However, being prepared and willing to go to trial is essential for maximizing your recovery. Insurance companies offer significantly more when they know your attorney is ready and able to take the case in front of a Houston jury.
