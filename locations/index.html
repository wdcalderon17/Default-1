Service Areas

Calderon Legal Group serves personal injury victims along the I-45 and US-290 corridors — from Houston to College Station and everywhere in between.

🏙

Houston

Our primary office serves all of Houston and Harris County. Car accidents, truck crashes, slip and fall, wrongful death — we fight for Houston injury victims every day.
📍

Katy

Serving Katy and Fort Bend/Harris County. If you were injured on I-10 or anywhere in the Katy area, call us for a free case evaluation.
🌿

The Woodlands

Personal injury representation for accident victims in The Woodlands, Spring, and Montgomery County along the I-45 corridor north of Houston.
📌

Conroe

Serving Conroe and Montgomery County. From I-45 crashes to workplace injuries, Calderon Legal Group is here for Conroe-area injury victims.
📌

Huntsville

The I-45 corridor through Huntsville sees heavy truck traffic and frequent accidents. We represent Walker County injury victims at no upfront cost.

Bryan / College Station

Serving the Brazos Valley including Bryan, College Station, and the Texas A&M area. Local personal injury representation along the US-290 corridor.
