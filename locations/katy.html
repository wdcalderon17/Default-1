Personal Injury Lawyers in Katy, TX

Calderon Legal Group represents injury victims in Katy and the surrounding Fort Bend and Harris County communities. If you've been hurt in an accident on I-10, the Grand Parkway, or anywhere in the Katy area, our attorneys are ready to fight for your full compensation.

Katy's Personal Injury Law Firm

Katy is one of the fastest-growing communities in Texas and the United States. The city of Katy itself is home to more than 22,000 residents, but the broader Katy area — spanning Harris, Fort Bend, and Waller counties — encompasses a metro population of more than 300,000 people. This explosive growth has brought with it new roads, new commercial development, and unfortunately, a significant increase in traffic accidents and injuries.

Calderon Legal Group serves injury victims throughout the Katy area. Whether you were hurt in a rear-end collision on the I-10 Katy Freeway, a T-bone crash at a Grand Parkway intersection, or a slip and fall at a Katy retail center, our personal injury attorneys are ready to fight for you.

Katy's Most Dangerous Roads

Rapid population growth in the Katy area has placed enormous strain on local roads. TxDOT data and local crash reports consistently identify these roads as among the most dangerous in the region:

  • I-10 (Katy Freeway) — one of the widest and most heavily traveled freeways in the world, with frequent high-speed collisions and significant commercial truck traffic
  • Grand Parkway (SH-99) — a rapidly expanding tollway that sees increasing accident rates as development pushes outward from Houston
  • FM 1093 (Westheimer Parkway) — a key east-west connector through the Katy area with high traffic volume and multiple serious intersection crashes
  • Mason Road — a busy north-south corridor through central Katy with commercial and residential traffic mixing at high volumes
  • Cinco Ranch Boulevard — serves the massive Cinco Ranch master-planned community and sees significant pedestrian and school-area traffic
  • Fry Road — a high-volume north-south connector with a documented history of intersection accidents

Why Katy Drivers Need a Personal Injury Attorney

Katy-area accident victims often deal with multiple insurance companies across different counties — Harris County, Fort Bend County, and sometimes Waller County. This jurisdictional complexity, combined with the fact that many Katy-area accidents involve commercial vehicles using I-10 as a freight route, means that liability and insurance issues are frequently complicated.

Calderon Legal Group understands both the Harris County and Fort Bend County court systems. FAQs: Personal Injury Claims in Katy, TX

It can matter for where your lawsuit would be filed. The Katy area spans Harris County, Fort Bend County, and portions of Waller County. The specific location of your accident determines the proper venue for litigation. Calderon Legal Group is experienced in the courts across all three counties and will ensure your case is filed in the appropriate jurisdiction.
Truck accident cases are significantly more complex than standard car accident claims. Commercial trucks are subject to federal FMCSA regulations, and the trucking company's insurer will have experienced defense lawyers working from day one. Evidence like the truck's electronic logging device (ELD), black box data, and driver records must be preserved quickly. Contact Calderon Legal Group immediately after a truck accident so we can send a spoliation letter and preserve critical evidence.
Texas law generally gives injury victims two years from the date of the accident to file a personal injury lawsuit (Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code § 16.003). However, certain exceptions apply — claims involving government-owned vehicles or public entities may have much shorter notice requirements. Do not wait. Contact Calderon Legal Group for a free consultation as soon as possible after your accident.
