Conroe's Personal Injury Law Firm

Conroe is the county seat of Montgomery County and one of the fastest-growing cities in Texas. With a population exceeding 100,000 residents and serving as the governmental and commercial hub for the broader Montgomery County area, Conroe sits at an important crossroads along the I-45 corridor connecting Houston to points north.

Conroe's growth — and the growth of the surrounding area including The Woodlands and other Montgomery County communities — has brought significantly more traffic onto roads that were not originally designed for these volumes. Calderon Legal Group represents Conroe-area injury victims in all types of personal injury cases, from car and truck accidents to slip and fall injuries and wrongful death claims.

Conroe's Most Dangerous Roads

Several of Conroe's roadways consistently appear in accident reports and TxDOT crash data:

  • I-45 — Conroe sits on one of Texas's most dangerous interstate highways; the stretch through Montgomery County sees significant commuter and commercial truck traffic daily
  • SH-105 — a major east-west connector through Conroe that intersects I-45 and carries high volumes of mixed traffic into surrounding communities
  • SH-75 (North Frazier Street) — a primary commercial corridor through central Conroe with numerous intersection conflicts and pedestrian activity
  • FM 3083 — a growing north-south route connecting Conroe to new residential and commercial developments experiencing rapid increases in traffic volume
  • Loop 336 — Conroe's main bypass loop carries high traffic around the city and has seen significant accident activity, particularly at interchange points

Filing Your Case in Montgomery County

Personal injury cases arising from accidents in Conroe and Montgomery County are typically handled in the Montgomery County District Courts, located in Conroe — the county seat. Calderon Legal Group is thoroughly familiar with the Montgomery County court system, local judges, and the procedural requirements for filing injury claims in this jurisdiction.

Whether your case can be resolved through insurance settlement or requires litigation in the Montgomery County courts, our attorneys are prepared to handle every phase of your claim.
