Bryan–College Station Personal Injury Attorneys

The Bryan–College Station metropolitan area is home to Texas A&M University, one of the largest universities in the United States, along with a rapidly growing population of more than 270,000 residents across Brazos County. This growth brings increased traffic, construction, and unfortunately, accidents.

Calderon Legal Group serves injury victims throughout the Bryan-College Station area. Whether you were hurt in a car accident on Texas Avenue, a truck crash on SH-6, a slip and fall on the Texas A&M campus area, or any other accident, our attorneys are ready to fight for your maximum compensation.

Common Injury Cases in Bryan–College Station

  • Car accidents on Texas Avenue, SH-6, Harvey Road, University Drive, and FM 2818
  • Truck accidents on SH-6 and US-190 (the road connecting College Station to I-45)
  • Motorcycle accidents near the Texas A&M campus
  • Premises liability and slip and fall in Bryan and College Station stores, restaurants, and properties
  • Wrongful death claims for Brazos County families
  • Pedestrian and bicycle accidents near the Texas A&M campus
  • DWI and drunk driving accidents

Dangerous Roads in the Bryan–College Station Area

  • Texas Avenue (SH-6 Business) — the main commercial corridor through both cities; high-volume traffic and frequent crashes
  • SH-6 Bypass — heavy commercial truck traffic connecting College Station to Houston
  • Harvey Road / University Drive — busy corridors near Texas A&M with significant pedestrian and student traffic
  • FM 2818 (Earl Rudder Freeway) — high-speed connector road with significant crash history
  • US-190 / SH-21 — connecting the Brazos Valley to I-45 and points west

Texas A&M Campus Area Accidents

With over 70,000 students, faculty, and staff, the area surrounding Texas A&M University sees a high volume of pedestrian, bicycle, and vehicle traffic. Student drivers unfamiliar with local roads, and heavy game-day traffic during football season, contribute to elevated accident rates in Aggieland.

If you or a family member was injured in an accident near Texas A&M or anywhere in Brazos County, Calderon Legal Group can help. We handle cases involving injuries to students, residents, and visitors alike.

Why Hire a Personal Injury Attorney for Your Bryan–College Station Case?

Many injury victims in the College Station area make the mistake of handling their own insurance claims. Insurance companies — including the large regional insurers serving Central Texas — have experienced adjusters and attorneys working against you from day one.
