I-45 and Huntsville Personal Injury Attorneys

Huntsville, Texas — the county seat of Walker County and home of Sam Houston State University — sits at the midpoint of the I-45 corridor connecting Houston to Dallas. This positioning makes it a high-traffic zone for commercial trucks, highway travelers, and local drivers alike.

The stretch of I-45 running through Walker County consistently ranks among the most dangerous segments of highway in the entire state of Texas, with high rates of fatal crashes, particularly involving commercial trucks. If you've been injured on I-45 near Huntsville or anywhere in Walker County, Calderon Legal Group is ready to fight for you.

Why I-45 Near Huntsville Is So Dangerous

Several factors make the I-45 corridor through Huntsville especially hazardous:

  • Heavy commercial truck traffic — I-45 is a primary freight artery between Houston, Dallas, and beyond
  • High speeds — 75-mph posted limits with vehicles often traveling faster
  • Deer and wildlife crossings — the forested terrain of Walker County creates significant animal collision risk
  • Fatigue-related crashes — long-haul truck drivers on extended runs
  • Weather events — fog and rain in the pine forest corridor reduce visibility
  • Construction zones — TxDOT improvement projects create merge conflicts
