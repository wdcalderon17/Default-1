The Woodlands Personal Injury Law Firm

The Woodlands is one of the most successful master-planned communities in the United States, home to approximately 115,000 residents across its nine villages in southern Montgomery County. Originally developed by the Woodlands Corporation in 1974, The Woodlands has grown into a major economic and residential hub — with a thriving Town Center, corporate campuses for dozens of major companies, and a dense network of roads connecting its residents to Houston and surrounding communities.

As The Woodlands has grown, so have its traffic challenges. The area's main corridor — Interstate 45 — carries enormous volumes of commuter and commercial traffic daily. Calderon Legal Group represents injury victims throughout The Woodlands and the broader Montgomery County area in all types of personal injury cases.

The Woodlands' Most Dangerous Roads

Despite its planned design and manicured appearance, The Woodlands has several roads that generate significant accident numbers each year:

  • I-45 (North Freeway) — the primary corridor through The Woodlands, carrying heavy commuter and commercial truck traffic between Houston and points north
  • SH-242 — a major east-west connector that has seen rapid commercial growth and corresponding increases in traffic and accidents
  • Research Forest Drive — a key corridor through the corporate and research district, with high volumes of employee and delivery traffic
  • Woodlands Parkway — the main east-west spine of The Woodlands, lined with commercial development and high daily traffic counts
  • Gosling Road — a north-south connector serving multiple villages and experiencing growing accident rates
  • FM 1488 — a state farm-to-market road at the southern edge of The Woodlands that intersects with I-45 and carries heavy traffic
