Background & Education

William Diaz Calderon was born in Santo Domingo and raised in Columbus, Ohio — a journey that shaped his commitment to serving diverse communities and his deep understanding of the immigrant experience in America.

He earned his Bachelor of Arts in Public Administration from Miami University in Oxford, Ohio, before earning his Juris Doctor from Michigan State University College of Law. His academic background gave him a rigorous foundation in law, public policy, and government institutions that continues to inform his practice today.

Career as a Felony Prosecutor

Attorney Calderon began his legal career as a felony prosecutor for the State of Texas, representing the State in complex jury trials across multiple counties. This experience honed his trial skills, deepened his understanding of how cases are built and prosecuted, and gave him an insider's view of the criminal justice system that few defense attorneys possess.

Mr. Calderon has also been appointed by judges to advise attorneys regarding immigration consequences in criminal case dispositions — a recognition of his unique expertise at the intersection of criminal law and immigration law.

Fighting for Injury Victims, the Accused & Immigrant Families

Today, Attorney Calderon channels his prosecutorial experience into three primary practice areas:

  • Personal Injury: Representing accident victims across the I-45 corridor on a contingency fee basis — no win, no fee.
  • Criminal Defense: Using prosecutorial knowledge to provide vigorous defense for misdemeanor and felony charges. He has secured dismissals in serious felony matters and helped clients avoid jail time.
  • Immigration Law: Assisting individuals and families with DACA, family petitions, deportation defense, citizenship, and more — with special expertise when criminal charges intersect with immigration status.

Community & Languages

Attorney Calderon is fluent in English and Spanish and deeply committed to serving Houston's and Bryan–College Station's large Latino communities. He serves clients throughout Harris, Brazos, Montgomery, Fort Bend, Walker, Grimes, and Burleson Counties.
