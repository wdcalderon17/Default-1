Simple Process

How Your Personal Injury Case Works

We handle the legal complexity so you can focus on your recovery. Here's what to expect:

1

Free Consultation

Call, text, or fill out our form. We'll review your case for free and tell you honestly whether you have a claim — no obligations.

2

Investigation & Evidence

We gather police reports, medical records, surveillance footage, and witness statements to build the strongest case possible.

3

Demand & Negotiation

We send a demand package to the insurance company and negotiate aggressively for maximum compensation before any lawsuit.

4

Settlement or Trial

Most cases settle favorably. If the insurer won't pay what you deserve, we're prepared to take your case in front of a Texas jury.

5

You Get Paid

Once your case resolves, you receive your compensation. We handle all the paperwork and distributions so you don't have to.
