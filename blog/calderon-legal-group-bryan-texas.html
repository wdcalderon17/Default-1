Your Trusted Personal Injury Attorneys in Bryan, Texas

Calderon Legal Group brings full Houston-level resources and advocacy to injury victims throughout the Brazos Valley.

Why Bryan and College Station Need a Dedicated Injury Attorney

The Bryan-College Station area presents a unique set of road safety challenges. Texas A&M University enrolls more than 70,000 students, many of whom are young, inexperienced drivers navigating busy corridors for the first time. State Highway 6 carries a heavy mix of commercial truck traffic, commuters, and student drivers. Texas Avenue — the spine connecting Bryan and College Station — is lined with intersections that generate a disproportionate number of T-bone and rear-end collisions year-round.

Adding to the complexity, the area attracts a large volume of out-of-state and out-of-county visitors for football games, university events, and agricultural conventions. Drivers unfamiliar with local roads, traffic patterns, and highway on-ramps create real dangers for residents who use these roads every day. When a crash happens, the at-fault driver's insurer may be headquartered across the country, making local legal representation even more critical.

We maintain deep familiarity with the 85th District Court and the 272nd District Court in Brazos County — the two courts where most personal injury cases in the area are litigated — and we understand how local judges and juries evaluate injury claims.

Personal Injury Cases We Handle in Bryan-College Station

Calderon Legal Group represents Brazos Valley residents and visitors across the full spectrum of serious personal injury matters, including:

Getting Started With Your Brazos Valley Injury Claim

The first step is a free consultation. You can reach us by phone at (832) 261-5500 or by submitting your information through our online contact form. We respond quickly — often within the hour during business hours — and we can conduct your initial consultation by phone, video, or in person.

When you come to your consultation, it helps to bring whatever documentation you have: the police report (or its report number), photos from the scene, any medical records or bills you've received, insurance correspondence, and the other driver's insurance information. But don't worry if you don't have everything — we can obtain records and reports on your behalf.

After reviewing your situation, we'll explain your legal options clearly and in plain language, outline the potential value of your claim, discuss the timeline and process, and answer every question you have. If you decide to move forward, we'll get to work immediately — because in personal injury cases, time and evidence matter.
