About Calderon Legal Group

Dedicated personal injury attorneys fighting for injured Texans from Houston to College Station — and everywhere in between.

Our Mission

Fighting for Injured Texans Who Deserve a Powerful Advocate

At Calderon Legal Group, we believe that every person injured by someone else's negligence — regardless of their background, income, or immigration status — deserves access to experienced, aggressive legal representation. The insurance companies that you face after an accident have armies of lawyers and adjusters working against you. We're here to make sure you have someone equally powerful fighting for you.

We founded Calderon Legal Group to serve the communities along the Houston-to-College Station corridor: the families in Katy, the workers in Conroe, the students in Bryan and College Station, the truck drivers and motorists on I-45 and US-290, and the millions of residents of Greater Houston. These are our neighbors — and when they're hurt, we fight for them.

Our Service Area

Serving the Houston–College Station Corridor

Calderon Legal Group serves personal injury clients throughout Southeast and Central Texas. Our primary service area spans the I-45 and US-290 corridors from Houston to College Station, including:

  • Greater Houston — all of Harris County and surrounding communities
  • Fort Bend County — Katy, Sugar Land, Missouri City
  • Montgomery County — The Woodlands, Conroe, Magnolia
  • Waller County — Hempstead, Brookshire
  • Walker County — Huntsville, New Waverly
  • Grimes County — Navasota, Anderson
  • Washington County — Brenham, Chappell Hill
  • Brazos County — Bryan, College Station
