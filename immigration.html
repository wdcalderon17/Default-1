Immigration Services We Provide

We assist individuals, families, and employers with a full range of immigration matters throughout Texas from our offices in Houston and Bryan–College Station.

Family & Spousal Petitions

We help U.S. citizens and lawful permanent residents bring immediate family members to the United States through Form I-130 and related filings, guiding you through every step from initial filing through visa issuance or adjustment of status.

DACA Renewals

We prepare and file Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals (DACA) renewal applications to help recipients maintain their protection from deportation and their work authorization. We stay current on all federal developments affecting the DACA program.

Deportation Defense & Cancellation of Removal

Our attorneys represent clients in immigration court, filing motions to terminate, seeking cancellation of removal, asylum, withholding of removal, and other forms of relief. We have helped numerous clients avoid deportation and secure the right to remain in the United States.

Citizenship & Naturalization

If you are a lawful permanent resident who meets the requirements, we assist with your N-400 Application for Naturalization — preparing you for the interview and guiding you to becoming a U.S. citizen.

Temporary Protected Status (TPS)

We help eligible individuals apply for and maintain TPS, including renewals and re-registration periods for nationals of designated countries.

Immigration Bond Hearings

When a loved one is detained by ICE, we act quickly to request a bond hearing before an immigration judge to seek release while the case proceeds.

Student Visas (F-1) & OPT

We assist international students with F-1 visa applications, OPT, CPT, and maintaining lawful status — particularly important given our proximity to Texas A&M University in College Station.

H-1B & Employer-Sponsored Visas

We represent employers and employees in H-1B specialty occupation petitions, labor condition applications, and other employment-based immigration matters for Houston-area businesses.

Waivers

We evaluate your situation and file appropriate waivers — including the I-601A provisional unlawful presence waiver — to help you overcome grounds of inadmissibility arising from prior removal, unlawful presence, or criminal history.

Immigration Consequences of Criminal Charges

Attorney Calderon has been appointed by judges to advise attorneys on immigration consequences in criminal case dispositions. This unique credential means we can:

  • Advise non-citizen defendants and their criminal defense attorneys on how a plea or conviction affects immigration status
  • Negotiate plea agreements that minimize immigration consequences
  • Handle both the criminal defense and immigration case simultaneously — keeping the full picture in view

Crimes involving moral turpitude, aggravated felonies, drug offenses, and domestic violence can trigger deportation or permanent bars to re-entry. Contact us before accepting any plea deal if you have immigration concerns.

Immigration FAQ

DACA (Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals) grants temporary protection from deportation and work authorization to individuals brought to the U.S. as children who meet certain requirements. Recipients can apply for two-year renewals. The program has faced ongoing legal challenges; we stay current on all developments and help clients navigate their options.
An arrest alone generally does not bar you from immigration benefits, though it must be disclosed. However, certain convictions — crimes involving moral turpitude, aggravated felonies, drug offenses, and domestic violence — can make you inadmissible or deportable. This is why it's critical to consult with an attorney who understands both criminal and immigration law before accepting any plea deal.
Processing times vary widely. Immediate relatives of U.S. citizens (spouse, minor children, parents) have no annual cap and often complete the process in 12–24 months. Preference category petitions (adult children, siblings) can take years or even decades due to annual visa limits and country backlogs. We review your specific situation and provide a realistic timeline during your consultation.
