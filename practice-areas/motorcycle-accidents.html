Houston Motorcycle Accident Lawyers

Motorcyclists deserve the road. When another driver's negligence takes that from you, Calderon Legal Group fights for every dollar you deserve.

Advocates for Motorcycle Riders Across Greater Houston

Motorcyclists face unique dangers on Houston's roads. Drivers often fail to check blind spots, misjudge speeds, or simply don't see riders — especially in heavy Houston traffic. When crashes happen, motorcycle riders suffer catastrophic injuries: broken bones, road rash, spinal cord damage, traumatic brain injury, and worse.

To make matters worse, insurance companies often try to blame motorcycle riders for accidents — even when another driver was clearly at fault. Calderon Legal Group combats this bias and fights aggressively to maximize compensation for injured riders throughout Harris County, Montgomery County, and Brazos County.

Common Causes of Houston Motorcycle Accidents

  • Drivers making left turns into motorcycles' path
  • Failure to yield at intersections
  • Unsafe lane changes and merging into a rider's lane
  • Distracted drivers (phone use, texting)
  • Following too closely (tailgating)
  • Opening car doors into a motorcycle's path ("dooring")
  • Drunk or impaired driving
  • Speeding
  • Road hazards — potholes, uneven pavement, debris

Injuries Common in Houston Motorcycle Crashes

  • Traumatic brain injury (TBI)
  • Spinal cord injuries and paralysis
  • Road rash and severe lacerations
  • Fractures — especially arms, legs, and collarbones
  • Internal organ damage
  • Amputation
  • PTSD and psychological trauma

Frequently Asked Questions

Texas requires all motorcycle riders under 21 to wear a helmet. Riders 21 and older may ride without a helmet if they have completed a safety course or have qualifying health insurance. Even if you were not wearing a helmet, you may still recover compensation — though the at-fault party may argue your lack of a helmet contributed to your head injuries. Our attorneys are experienced handling these arguments.
Lane splitting (riding between lanes of slow or stopped traffic) is not expressly permitted in Texas and can affect liability arguments. However, it doesn't automatically bar your recovery. Our attorneys examine all facts, witness statements, and evidence to establish an accurate account of the crash.
You can pursue medical expenses (including long-term rehabilitation), lost wages, future lost earning capacity, pain and suffering, disfigurement, and loss of enjoyment of life. In severe cases involving permanent disability, settlements and verdicts can be substantial. We work with medical and economic experts to fully document your damages.
