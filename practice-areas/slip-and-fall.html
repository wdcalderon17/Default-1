Slip & Fall Injury Lawyers Serving Houston

Property owners in Texas have a legal duty to keep their premises safe. When they fail and you're injured, Calderon Legal Group holds them accountable.

Houston Premises Liability & Slip and Fall Attorneys

Slip and fall accidents are among the most common causes of serious personal injury in Texas. Every year, thousands of Texans are injured in Houston grocery stores, parking lots, restaurants, shopping centers, apartment complexes, and other properties due to negligent conditions that property owners failed to address.

Under Texas premises liability law, property owners and occupiers owe a duty of care to visitors. When they breach that duty — by failing to clean up a spill, fix broken flooring, repair poor lighting, or warn of known hazards — and someone is hurt, they can be held liable.

Common Hazards in Premises Liability Cases

  • Wet or slippery floors without warning signs
  • Uneven or cracked sidewalks and pavement
  • Broken or missing handrails
  • Poor or broken lighting in stairwells and parking areas
  • Loose rugs, carpeting, or mats
  • Merchandise or debris left in walkways
  • Potholes in parking lots
  • Unmarked elevation changes

Proving a Slip and Fall Case in Texas

To win a premises liability case in Texas, you generally need to prove:

  1. The property owner/occupier owned, occupied, or controlled the premises
  2. A hazardous condition existed on the property
  3. The owner knew or reasonably should have known about the hazard
  4. The owner failed to fix the hazard or adequately warn visitors
  5. The hazard caused your injury and resulting damages

Evidence in these cases includes surveillance video (which property owners may delete quickly), incident reports, maintenance logs, witness statements, and medical documentation. Our attorneys act fast to preserve this critical evidence.
