Houston Brain Injury Lawyers

Brain injuries are among the most catastrophic consequences of accidents in Houston. Our TBI attorneys understand the long-term medical and financial impact — and fight to recover every dollar you deserve.

What Is a Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI)?

A traumatic brain injury occurs when a sudden blow, jolt, or penetrating injury to the head disrupts normal brain function. TBIs range widely in severity — from mild concussions that resolve within weeks to severe injuries causing permanent cognitive impairment, paralysis, or death.

In Houston, TBIs are a tragically common consequence of the city's high-volume traffic and dangerous work environments. Whether the result of a highway crash on I-45 or a fall at a construction site, a TBI can permanently alter the victim's life and the lives of their family members. The medical expenses, rehabilitation costs, and lost income can be overwhelming without proper legal representation.

Common Causes of Brain Injuries We Handle

  • Car accidents on I-45 and I-10 — high-speed collisions are a leading cause of TBIs in the Houston area
  • Truck accidents — the force of an 18-wheeler collision frequently causes severe head trauma
  • Slip and fall accidents — falls on unsafe premises, especially head-first impacts on hard surfaces
  • Motorcycle crashes — even helmeted riders can suffer serious TBIs in collisions
  • Workplace accidents — falling objects, falls from heights, and equipment accidents on Houston job sites
  • Pedestrian and bicycle accidents — vulnerable road users face extreme TBI risk when struck by vehicles
  • Sports and recreational accidents — including negligently supervised activities

Symptoms and Long-Term Effects of TBI

Brain injury symptoms may not be immediately apparent after an accident. Some TBI victims feel fine at first, only to develop serious symptoms hours or days later. Common symptoms and long-term effects include:

  • Persistent headaches and migraines
  • Memory loss and difficulty forming new memories
  • Cognitive impairment — trouble concentrating, problem-solving, and processing information
  • Personality changes, mood swings, depression, and anxiety
  • Seizures and epilepsy
  • Speech and language difficulties (aphasia)
  • Sensory problems — vision loss, hearing loss, loss of smell or taste
  • Motor deficits — weakness, loss of coordination, paralysis
  • Sleep disorders
  • Permanent disability requiring ongoing care

If you or a loved one experienced any head impact in an accident, seek immediate medical evaluation — even if symptoms seem mild. Early diagnosis protects both your health and your legal claim.

Compensation Available for Brain Injury Victims

TBI victims and their families may be entitled to substantial compensation, including:

  • Extensive medical bills — emergency care, hospitalization, surgery, imaging (CT scans, MRIs), and specialist treatment
  • Future medical care costs — ongoing neurological treatment, physical therapy, occupational therapy, and speech therapy
  • Lost earning capacity — compensation for reduced ability to work now and in the future
  • Pain and suffering — physical pain and emotional distress from the injury
  • Loss of enjoyment of life — inability to participate in activities and relationships that were meaningful before the injury
  • Caretaker and home care costs — professional nursing care, home health aides, and family caregiver compensation
  • Home modification costs — wheelchair ramps, accessible bathrooms, and other necessary adaptations
  • Punitive damages — in cases of gross negligence or intentional misconduct

Why TBI Cases Require an Experienced Attorney

Brain injury cases are among the most legally and medically complex personal injury claims. Insurance companies routinely minimize TBIs — especially mild-to-moderate cases — because the injuries are not always visible on standard imaging. Insurers may argue your symptoms are exaggerated, pre-existing, or unrelated to the accident.

At Calderon Legal Group, we work with neurologists, neuropsychologists, and life care planners to document the full extent of your injury and calculate the true lifetime cost of your care. We build cases that withstand insurance company challenges and, if necessary, take them to trial.

Future damages calculations are particularly critical in TBI cases. A settlement that seems large today may be wholly inadequate to cover decades of medical care and lost income. Our attorneys ensure that every future need is accounted for before any settlement is accepted.
