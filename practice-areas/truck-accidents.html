Houston Truck & 18-Wheeler Accident Lawyers

Catastrophic injuries caused by commercial trucks deserve serious legal representation. Our attorneys take on the trucking industry's legal teams and insurers — and win.

Fighting for Truck Accident Victims Along I-45, I-10 & US-290

Texas has more registered commercial vehicles than any other state, and the I-45 corridor between Houston and College Station is one of the most heavily traveled trucking routes in the country. When an 18-wheeler, semi-truck, or other large commercial vehicle collides with a passenger car, the results are often catastrophic or fatal.

Calderon Legal Group has the experience and resources to take on large trucking companies and their powerful insurers. We act quickly to preserve critical evidence, interview witnesses, and build the strongest possible case on your behalf.

  • Delivery truck and FedEx/UPS accidents

Why Truck Accident Cases Are Complex

Commercial truck accidents are dramatically more complex than standard car accident cases. They involve:

  • Federal FMCSA regulations governing driver hours, weight limits, maintenance, and drug testing
  • Multiple potential defendants: the driver, the trucking company, the cargo loader, the leasing company, and the manufacturer
  • Black-box (ECM) data showing speed, braking, and engine performance at the time of crash
  • Electronic logging devices (ELD) documenting hours of service violations
  • Driver qualification files and employment records
  • Large insurance policies — and insurers who deploy teams of lawyers immediately

This is why you must act fast. Trucking companies begin investigating immediately after an accident. Evidence disappears. You need an attorney who will match their urgency — and that's exactly what we do.

Frequently Asked Questions — Truck Accidents

Multiple parties may be liable: the truck driver, the trucking company, the cargo loader, the truck manufacturer (if a defect contributed), and others. Our attorneys conduct a thorough investigation to identify every responsible party and maximize your recovery.
Truck accident cases involve federal FMCSA regulations, multiple defendants, ELD data, black-box data, driver qualification files, and large commercial insurance policies. Speed matters — trucking companies send investigators to the scene immediately. You need an experienced attorney in your corner just as fast.
Trucking companies routinely attempt to shift blame. Our investigators and accident reconstruction experts review all available evidence — surveillance video, ELD data, maintenance records, driver logs, cell phone records — to prove liability. We don't take their word for it.
Truck accident settlements are often significantly higher than car accident settlements because injuries tend to be more severe and commercial insurance policies carry higher limits. Compensation can include medical expenses (including future care), lost wages and earning capacity, pain and suffering, disability, disfigurement, and in some cases punitive damages.
