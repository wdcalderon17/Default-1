Personal Injury Practice Areas

Calderon Legal Group represents injury victims along the Houston-to-College Station corridor. Select your case type to learn how we can help.

🚗

Car Accidents

Injured in a car crash on I-45, I-10, or anywhere in the Houston area? We fight to maximize your recovery for medical bills, lost wages, and pain and suffering.
🚐

Truck Accidents

Commercial truck crashes cause catastrophic injuries. Our attorneys understand federal trucking regulations and how to hold carriers accountable.
🚘

Motorcycle Accidents

Motorcyclists face unique dangers on Texas roads. We fight the bias against riders and pursue full compensation for your injuries.
⚠

Slip & Fall

Property owners owe a duty of care to visitors. If unsafe conditions caused your injury, we can help you recover under Texas premises liability law.
⚔

Wrongful Death

Losing a family member to someone else's negligence is devastating. We pursue justice and financial security for surviving families.
🛠

Work Injuries

Injured on the job? We help workers navigate Texas workers' comp and pursue third-party claims when available.
🧠

Brain Injury

Traumatic brain injuries carry lifelong consequences. We build comprehensive cases to secure maximum compensation for TBI victims and their families.
💔

Birth Injury

Medical negligence during labor and delivery can cause permanent harm. We fight for families whose children suffered preventable birth injuries.
🚕

Rideshare Accidents

Uber and Lyft accident claims involve complex insurance layers. Our attorneys navigate coverage disputes to maximize your recovery.
📜

Uninsured Motorist

Hit by a driver with no insurance? We help you file a UM/UIM claim against your own policy to recover the compensation you deserve.
🚌

Public Transportation

Claims against government transit agencies follow special rules in Texas. We know the deadlines and procedures to protect your rights.
